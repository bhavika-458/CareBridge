Core UX Principle

"Normal should be simple. Problems should reveal detail."

CareBridge is thoughtfully made for sons, daughters, family caregivers, and non-medical staff. Most days don't require digging through complex medical charts. You get an immediate daily checkup; when something unusual is observed, reporting and escalating it to clinical staff is seamless.

Everything you need, in one place

Simple tools for daily peace of mind

Five essentials designed to keep care clear, organized, and accessible on any iPad, tablet, or mobile phone.

1. Today's Care

A quick morning checkup showing mood, meals, hydration, and overall status in a few seconds.

2. Medicines

Clear morning, afternoon, and evening timelines. Only see what is due today without confusing pharmacology terms.

3. Behavior & Concerns

Non-alarming observation reporting. If something feels off, easily record it for care staff to review.

4. Documents

Insurance cards, care plans, and doctor packets stored quietly until you actually need them.

5. Doctor Visits

Keep upcoming appointment dates, physician notes, and questions in one easily accessible list.
